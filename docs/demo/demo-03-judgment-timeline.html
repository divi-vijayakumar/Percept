04 · How to read this
Each track is one case on its media clock. The green band is the annotated ground-truth window; black marks are Incidents inside it, red marks are Incidents outside it. The thin bar underneath is that case's gate split — fire / ask / silent — across every judged unit.

A healthy case shows a small number of black marks inside the band and little else. A case that is mostly amber is asking rather than deciding; a case with many red marks is firing on the wrong moments. Both fail differently, and an aggregate recall number hides which one you have.
