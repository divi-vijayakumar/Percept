### Added — `SubjectRef`: a namespaced, tri-state subject identity (contract 1.4.0)

The SDK could perceive a document/entity correctly but could not *say which one* — a failure exposed
by domain probes. `percept.contracts.SubjectRef` makes identity a first-class, namespaced,
honestly-uncertain value. Additive and GATE-IRRELEVANT: `watch.py` and `WATCH_VERSION` are FROZEN;
the gate still reads only `satisfied`/`confidence`, so a judge that emits no subject reproduces
today's firing byte-for-byte. Contract semver `1.3.0` → `1.4.0` on both edges.

- **`SubjectRef(scheme, namespace, key, resolution, scope, strategy, confidence=None)`** — an OPEN
  `scheme` vocabulary (`track`/`content`/`slot`/client-defined), a `namespace` that disambiguates a
  key that is *not* globally unique (`PO-4471` means different things across ERPs), and TWO
  resolution states only: `bound` or `unknown`. Invariants enforced in `__post_init__`: `bound`
  requires a non-empty namespace AND key; `unknown` requires `key is None` (an unreadable key is
  rejected if a key is present — it is not a fabricated identity). The ABSENCE of a subject is
  `SubjectRef | None` == `None`, never a third resolution value.
- **Equality is identity, not provenance.** Two `unknown` refs NEVER compare equal (NaN-like — an
  unidentified subject is not "the same" as another, nor as itself); two `bound` refs are equal iff
  their `(scheme, namespace, key)` triple matches. `scope`/`strategy`/`confidence` never affect
  equality. `confidence` is deliberately NOT defaulted to `1.0` — a judge-emitted key is not certain
  by construction.
- **`scope='durable'` documents IDENTIFIER stability ONLY** — it is explicitly NOT a claim that
  episode state is persisted. percept's episode/continuity state is session-only.
- **`resolve_subject(client=, judge=, track=, unknown=)` → `SubjectResolution`** implements the
  precedence client-supplied bound > judge-emitted bound > track identity > unknown. When two BOUND
  resolvers disagree on the key it NEVER silently picks one: it returns the deterministic
  higher-precedence winner AND records each disagreement as a first-class `SubjectConflict`
  (`SubjectResolution.has_conflict`).
- **`Verdict.subject: SubjectRef | None = None`** (appended last, additive). `episodes._subject_match`
  now prefers a bound `verdict.subject.key` over the judge-match fallback, so a judge-emitted identity
  drives continuation without a judge call — and two same-region/same-label documents with different
  keys open two episodes instead of collapsing into one.
- **JS twin** `makeSubjectRef`/`parseSubjectRef` in `percept-edge/src/contract.js` mirrors the
  invariants byte-for-byte; the JSON schema is regenerated (`SubjectRef.schema.json`, `Verdict`
  refs it). Contract parity is asserted at the source level.
