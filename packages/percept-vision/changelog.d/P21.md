### Added — deterministic video-interest intervals

Programmable Watches over live multimodal streams can now turn a caller-selected scalar admission
signal into typed `interest.entered` / `interest.updated` / `interest.exited` intervals with stable
replay-deterministic ids. Events carry source and observer bounds, reason, score, optional calibrated
confidence, coverage, region/track metadata when supplied, and the admission/detector identities.

`InterestIntervalEngine` makes hysteresis, consecutive-sample debounce and idle expiry explicit.
Reconnect, Watch expiry and end-of-stream close an interval with distinct reasons; a silence gap at
the expiry boundary splits rather than silently forward-filling. Invalid/out-of-order evidence is
rejected before state changes, and the lifecycle cannot emit an orphan update/exit or duplicate enter.

`Tier0MotionInterest` is an opt-in view over the tier-0 contract's reported mean-motion gauge and is
wired into `GatedWatchLoop`/`LoopReport`. It intentionally does not treat `decision.escalate` as
semantic truth: localized, structural, semantic, audio and fused admission details cannot be
reconstructed from that boolean. Motion confidence therefore remains explicitly unset, and callers
must provide thresholds calibrated for their signal; no universal quality bar is embedded.
