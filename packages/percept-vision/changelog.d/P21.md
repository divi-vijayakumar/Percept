### Added — deterministic video-interest intervals

Finite tier-0 replay traces can now turn a caller-selected scalar admission signal into typed
`interest.entered` / `interest.updated` / `interest.exited` intervals with stable
replay-deterministic ids. Events carry distinct source and observer bounds, an explicit caller-owned
stream epoch, evidence id, reason, score, optional calibrated confidence, coverage, region/track
metadata when supplied, and the admission/detector identities.

`InterestIntervalEngine` makes hysteresis, consecutive-sample debounce and idle expiry explicit.
Reconnect, Watch expiry and end-of-stream close an interval with distinct reasons; a silence gap at
the expiry boundary splits rather than silently forward-filling. Invalid/out-of-order evidence is
rejected before state changes, and the lifecycle cannot emit an orphan update/exit or duplicate enter.

`Tier0MotionInterest` is a replay-only view over the tier-0 contract's reported mean-motion gauge and
is wired into the finite-iterable `GatedWatchLoop`/`LoopReport` harness, not `percept.run`. Every
successful run supplies an explicit epoch plus independent observer timestamps and closes at EOS;
failures discard the unreturned epoch without manufacturing an orphan exit, and the adapter is ready
for the next chunk. Each epoch cold-starts and ends with a reset tier-0 predecessor, so a frame from
another epoch cannot manufacture motion; motion evidence ids hash the exact ordered
`previous,current` frame pair rather than the current frame alone. It intentionally does not treat
`decision.escalate` as semantic truth: localized, structural, semantic, audio and fused admission
details cannot be reconstructed from that boolean. Motion confidence remains explicitly unset, and
callers must provide thresholds calibrated for their signal; no universal quality bar is embedded.
